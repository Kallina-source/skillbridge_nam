# skillbridge_nam
A digital gig and freelance marketplace for Namibian students and youth.

SkillBridge

Your skills. Your opportunity.

SkillBridge is a digital gig and freelance marketplace built specifically for Namibian students and youth, connecting them to flexible, short-term income opportunities while helping employers find reliable, affordable talent fast.

---

The Problem

Namibia has one of the highest youth unemployment rates in the world. Many students depend on NSFAS funding that is often delayed or insufficient, leading to missed classes, unpaid rent, and financial instability. At the same time, employers and individuals constantly need temporary workers, event staff, freelancers, and service providers, and have no structured way to find them beyond WhatsApp groups and Facebook posts.

The gap is clear. SkillBridge fills it.

---

The Solution

SkillBridge is a centralized platform where students and youth can discover gigs, apply for temporary work, and offer their freelance services. Employers can post opportunities, browse talent, and hire quickly. Communities benefit from structured, accountable, and transparent informal employment.

---

Who It's For

Students and Youth — Gig listings, freelance marketplace, profile and portfolio
Employers and Event Hosts — Post gigs, manage applicants, rate workers
Freelancers — Showcase skills, get discovered, build reputation

---

MVP Features

- User registration and login (Student / Employer)
- Student profile with skills, availability, and bio
- Employer profile
- Gig posting and browsing
- Application system
- Basic dashboard for both user types
- Category filtering (Events, Tutoring, Creative, Cleaning, and more)

---
